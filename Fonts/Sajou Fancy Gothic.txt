
Sajou Fancy Gothic is based on an unnamed alphabet on pages 3 and 4 of 
Sajou No. 236, a late 19th to early 20th century French embroidery booklet. 
The scan of Sajou was made available by Ramzi of Pattern Maker Charts at 
http://patternmakercharts.blogspot.com

Thanks to Sandra Rose for leading us to Ramzi's blog, to Graham Meade for 
suggestions on how to do the missing letter W and to Sharon, moderator of 
annexcafe.fonts, for naming the font. And, of course, to Ramzi for making 
available the scans.
